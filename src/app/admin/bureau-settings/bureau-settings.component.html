Credit Bureau Provider Settings

Choose whether CIBIL, CRIF, Experian and Equifax report fetches use Verifyal, Surepass or Satmat — for one account, or applied to every account at once. Surepass is live today for CIBIL and CRIF only; Experian and Equifax can be set now but stay on Verifyal until Surepass support is added for them. Satmat is live for CIBIL (TransUnion), CRIF and Experian; Satmat has no Equifax API, so choosing Satmat for Equifax disables the Equifax card for that account. Report costs are managed elsewhere and are not changed from this page.
