Credit Bureau Provider Settings

Choose whether CIBIL, CRIF, Experian and Equifax report fetches use Verifyal, Surepass or Satmat — for one account, or applied to every account at once. Surepass is live for CIBIL and CRIF only; choosing Surepass for Experian or Equifax disables that card in the CRM. Satmat is live for CIBIL (TransUnion), CRIF and Experian; Satmat has no Equifax API, so choosing Satmat for Equifax disables the Equifax card for that account. AV Management is live for all four bureaus and charges for every hit (failed requests included). Report costs are managed elsewhere and are not changed from this page.
